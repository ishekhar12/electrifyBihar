Who Are We?

Electrify Bihar is a non-profit organization established to invigorate electric vehicle adoption across Bihar, India.

While EV adoption is rapidly accelerating globally, regions in India are working to catch up to reduce greenhouse emissions. Our mission starts with a focused goal: driving EV adoption in the public sector within one of India's most critical regions.

"We provide FREE charging services to e-Rickshaw drivers—helping micro-entrepreneurs save on fuel costs while reducing urban air pollution."

🛺

Why e-Rickshaws?

e-Rickshaws are essential for affordable local transit. By supporting low-income drivers with free charging, we help replace fossil-fuel vehicles (petrol tempos) with clean, electric alternatives.

Our Services & Infrastructure

Expanding public EV charging infrastructure in Darbhanga and surrounding areas.

Level 2 AC Charging

AC2 7.5 kW charging stations compatible with 4-wheeler EVs (such as Tata Nexon EV) charging at approximately 41 km/hour.

Battery Swap & Fast Charge
